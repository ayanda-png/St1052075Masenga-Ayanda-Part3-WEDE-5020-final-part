About Us

Hakeem's Ready Set Clean is a small business from Mamelodi. It was founded in 2025 by Hakeem Khoza, a young entrepreneur who is also from Mamelodi.

The business specialises in professional cleaning services for homes, offices, schools and apartments. We offer affordable and reliable cleaning that people can trust.
